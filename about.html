ABOUT “THE PUBLIC ELEMENT”

The Public Element is a civic engagement resource designed to help students and young people better understand how government, advocacy, and community action work.

The goal is simple: make civic participation easier to understand, more approachable, and more useful in everyday life. Whether you want to learn how local government works, advocate for an issue, contact elected officials, organize on campus, or become more involved in your community, The Public Element is here to help you take that next step.

THE AUTHOR

The Public Element was created to make civic engagement easier to understand and more accessible to students and young adults.

Through experience in student leadership, advocacy, community involvement, and public service, the author developed this site as a place where people can learn about civic participation and find practical ways to get involved.
